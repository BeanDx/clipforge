## What's new

### 🥔 Potato graphics
- New look in **Effects → Graphics**: **Potato**. No lighting or shadows, flat walls and blurry textures, like the meme CS2 highlights on TikTok.
- It is only on while recording. Your CS2 settings stay as they are.

### 📈 FPS tab
- **FPS test**: CS2 runs 2 minutes of your demo at full speed and shows average FPS, 1% low and frame time. Change something, test again on the same demo and compare.
- **Hardware**: checks whether your RAM runs at its rated speed (XMP / EXPO) and in dual channel. In CS2 that is often 15–30% FPS.
- **CS2 graphics** with presets and what each setting costs in FPS. **Restore mine** puts your own settings back.
- **Background apps** that eat CPU and GPU right now, with a button to close them, plus a short Windows check (power plan, Game Mode, Game Bar capture, refresh rate).

### 🔧 HLAE updates itself
- After a CS2 update an old HLAE crashes on launch ("Problem in ... addresses.cpp"). clipforge now checks for a new HLAE on start and before every render and downloads it by itself. No need to install anything by hand.

### Fixes
- Hints with a preview no longer hide under the **Back / Next** bar.
- The summary cards on the **Render** step line up in an even grid.
- During a render the chicken sits next to **Stop** with the progress, instead of dropping to the bottom of the window.
- Video previews in the **Gallery** no longer start on every tile the cursor passes over while scrolling.
