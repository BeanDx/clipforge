## What's new

### 🗕 Tray mode and start with Windows
- The close button can hide clipforge to the tray instead of quitting. In the tray it uses almost no resources: the window draws nothing, while match stats and highlight bookmarks keep recording.
- Click the tray icon to open the window, right-click it to quit.
- **Start with Windows**: clipforge starts straight into the tray, so no match is missed.
- Opening clipforge again while it's in the tray brings up the running window instead of a second copy.
- Both switches are in **Settings → Tray and startup**.

### ✅ System check before rendering
Before every render clipforge checks that it can actually finish:
- free disk space against the expected size of the recording;
- write access to the highlights folder;
- paths to `cs2.exe`, `HLAE.exe` and the demo;
- the GPU driver (missing or very old);
- whether the laptop is running on battery.

Problems that would break the render stop it with a clear message, the rest only warn and let you start anyway. **Settings → System check** shows the summary in advance.

### 👥 Steam friends and player comparison
- **Profile** has a friends panel: your Steam friends straight from this PC, or any player's friends from their Steam page. Open a friend's profile or compare with them in one click. If the list is private, clipforge tells you how to make it public.
- **Compare** two players side by side: FACEIT Elo, matches, win rate, K/D, ADR, recent form and the maps both have played, with who's ahead highlighted.

### Also
- **Home**: long lists (match history, session matches) show the last three and expand smoothly. The playtime chart got hour marks, average per day, the longest day and days played.
- The window opens larger (1400×760) and shrinks to fit smaller screens.

## Download
**`clipforge_0.7.0_x64-setup.exe`** for Windows 10/11 (the `.msi` does the same). If you have 0.6.0, it updates by itself.

> Not code-signed yet: if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## По-русски
- **Трей и автозапуск**: крестик может сворачивать clipforge в трей, где он почти не тратит ресурсы, а статистика матчей и закладки хайлайтов пишутся дальше. Можно запускать вместе с Windows сразу в трей. Повторный запуск открывает уже работающее окно.
- **Проверка системы перед рендером**: место на диске, доступ к папке хайлайтов, пути к игре и HLAE, драйвер видеокарты, работа от батареи. Серьёзные проблемы останавливают рендер, остальные только предупреждают.
- **Друзья Steam и сравнение**: в «Профиле» список друзей, из которого можно открыть профиль или сравнить игроков бок о бок по эло, винрейту, K/D, ADR и картам.
- **Главная**: длинные списки свёрнуты до трёх последних, у графика времени в игре появились среднее за день, самый долгий день и число дней в игре.
